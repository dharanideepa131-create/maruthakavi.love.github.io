05 — A LETTER

Dear Maruthakavi,

I don't know if I can put everything I feel into a website.

But I wanted to try.

You spend your time capturing beautiful things through your camera. I hope you know that, somewhere along the way, you became one of those beautiful things in my life.

I love the way you see the world. I love your passion. I love the little things about you that you probably don't even realize I notice.

And I love the memories we've created.

Maybe this website is a little silly. Maybe it's a little cheesy. But I made it because you're worth doing something a little crazy for.

So here's to the photographs you haven't taken yet. The places you haven't seen. And all the memories still waiting for us.

Keep chasing the light, photographer.

I'll be somewhere in the frame.

❤️
Someone who sees you differently.
